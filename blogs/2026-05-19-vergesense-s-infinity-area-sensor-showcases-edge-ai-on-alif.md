The Infinity Area Sensor is powered by the Alif Ensemble E3®, a family built around dual Arm Cortex-M55® cores with support for dedicated Arm Ethos-U55® NPUs. The E3 architecture combines high-performance RTOS control and AI/ML acceleration with low-power wake capabilities, user interface support, and networking in a single device.

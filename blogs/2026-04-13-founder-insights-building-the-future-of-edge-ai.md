Reza Kazerounian shares how Alif Semiconductor is advancing edge AI with ultra-low power microcontrollers, Arm-based NPUs, and scalable MCU architectures for embedded AI and generative AI at the edge. "From day one the vision was clear: we know how this market works, and we set out to build a better, more scalable platform—the “ultimate” microcontroller/microprocessor foundation for the edge. That’s the mission and the execution path."

SIMS is taking a different approach. Using on-device AI vision, the wearable alerts users when a person or object is approaching from behind, turning situational awareness into a proactive capability rather than a forensic one. The device was showcased at CES as a partner demonstration running on Alif Semiconductor® silicon, providing a practical example of wearable edge AI applied to a real-world safety challenge.

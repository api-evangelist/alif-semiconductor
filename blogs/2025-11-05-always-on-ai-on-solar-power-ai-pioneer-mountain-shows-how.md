What’s needed is human-like sensory intelligence – but without the humans. And that’s what Grenoble, France-based MountAIn has created with its AI end-to-end infrastructure. MountAIn’s technology is already in use: since July 2024, a successful pilot in the south of France has been monitoring for potential wildfires, using an array of AI-enabled smart cameras to detect smoke and trigger real-time alerts before fires escalate.

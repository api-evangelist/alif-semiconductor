# Alif Semiconductor

> Fabless semiconductor company (Pleasanton, CA; Grenoble, France) designing secure, AI/ML-enabled 32-bit microcontrollers and fusion processors for edge computing. Products pair Arm Cortex-M55 cores with dedicated Arm Ethos-U55 NPUs for hardware-accelerated on-device AI, autonomous aiPM power management, and a multilayered hardware Root of Trust. Alif publishes an embedded developer surface (SDK, CMSIS packs, reference manuals, tools) rather than a public web API.

## Products

- [Ensemble family](https://alifsemi.com/products/ensemble/): E1C, E1, E3, E4, E5, E6, E7, E8 — Edge AI and Edge AI + Gen AI microcontrollers/fusion processors
- [Balletto family](https://alifsemi.com/products/balletto/): B1 — Edge AI + wireless microcontroller
